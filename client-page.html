Urban Soul — August 2026

Prepared by ROOTED Insights (demo)
Performance summary

Revenue reached R48,750 in August, up 18.4% on July, across 127 orders and 243 units sold.

What worked

The Oversized Hoodie carried 48% of total revenue and was the clear driver of this month's growth.

What didn't work

The Graphic Cap saw more product views than any other item but converted far less often — a sign the price or photos may need testing.

Customer behaviour

Customers who bought the Oversized Hoodie were also likely to add the Boxy Tee to the same order.

Recommendation

Consider a hoodie-and-tee bundle, and test a lower price or new product photo on the Graphic Cap before ordering more stock.

Next month's focus

Track whether the bundle lifts average order value, and re-measure Graphic Cap conversion after the price test.
