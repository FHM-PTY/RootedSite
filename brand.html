Kobus van der Merwe · Founder & Head Patternmaker

Kobus van der Merwe · Founder Journey

BOKKIE TAILORS · SALT RIVER, CAPE TOWN
How It Started:

"We started in 2019 on a solitary vintage Singer sewing machine tucked away in an old Salt River brick warehouse. Frustrated by lightweight synthetic imports that tore after three washes, we set out to make clothes engineered like industrial equipment."

The Inspiration:

"Our inspiration comes from Southern African workwear roots—Karoo farm jackets, dockworker smocks from Table Bay, and mineral-washed heavy cotton duck that gains character with age rather than decaying."

What We Hope to Achieve:

"By partnering with ROOTED, we don't have to spend twelve hours a day answering Instagram DMs and queuing at couriers. We want to prove that premium, world-class garments can be manufactured right here in South Africa at scale."
