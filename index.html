ABOUT ROOTED

Discover local. Wear local.

ROOTED is a managed online marketplace for emerging South African clothing brands. Instead of each small brand struggling to build its own storefront, buy traffic, and manage domestic courier logistics, all ateliers sell through one consolidated platform.

What we hope to achieve:
1. Managed Selling: Enable artisans to focus strictly on craft while our team handles listing, stock storage, packaging, and nationwide delivery.
2. Shared Discovery: Create a natural cross-pollination flywheel where customers exploring one brand naturally discover peer designers across Cape Town, Johannesburg, Pretoria, and Durban.
3. Data as the Real Product: Turn every checkout into actionable telemetry for makers—giving local brands live insight into sell-through velocity, sizing demand, and production planning.
